# Organ Attack! Rules Checklist

## ✅ Turn Rules

* [ ] Each player gets **1 turn**.
* [ ] Play **1 card** on your turn.
* [ ] Draw back up to **5 cards** at the end of your turn.
* [ ] If you have **no playable cards**, you may discard **up to 2 Attack cards** instead. This **counts as your turn**.
* [ ] **Instant** cards do **NOT** count as your turn.

---

## ✅ Affliction Rules

* [ ] Most organs require **2 matching afflictions** to be destroyed.
* [ ] **Necrosis = 2 afflictions**.
* [ ] **REMOVE** cards destroy the specified organ immediately.
* [ ] Destroyed organs cannot be healed.

---

## ✅ Contagious (Instant)

* [ ] Can only be played **when someone afflicts you**.
* [ ] Can be played during another player's turn.
* [ ] Does **NOT** count as your turn.
* [ ] Reflects/spreads the affliction according to the card text.
* [ ] Can still be played even if the attack would destroy your last organ.

---

## ✅ Immunity Boost

* [ ] Can be played at almost any time in response to another player's card.
* [ ] Blocks the incoming card unless that card specifically bypasses it.
* [ ] Does **NOT** count as your turn.

---

## ✅ Cure Cards

* [ ] Remove afflictions from living organs.
* [ ] Cannot restore destroyed organs.

---

## ✅ Medical Miracle

* [ ] Must be played **before** the organ is destroyed.
* [ ] Cannot bring back an organ that's already gone.

---

## ✅ Sedate / Narcolepsy

* [ ] This card can be played on any player, even if they are already in sleep (sedated/narcoleptic).
* [ ] The player who plays the card must choose an opponent to put to sleep.
* [ ] A sedated player skips their turn. They cannot defend themselves but can be attacked.
* [ ] Follow the exact wording on the card for when the effect ends.

---

## ✅ Cryopreservation

* [ ] Frozen player can be attacked.
* [ ] Frozen player cannot attack.
* [ ] Follow the card text for when the frozen player returns to play.

---

## ✅ Organ Wild

* [ ] Requires **4 afflictions** to be destroyed.
* [ ] Necrosis counts as **2 afflictions**.
* [ ] REMOVE effects count according to the card's rules.

---

## ✅ Metastasis

* [ ] Must be played immediately after another Attack card.
* [ ] Follow the card text for where the extra affliction is placed.

---

## ✅ Poisoned

* [ ] Resolves immediately when drawn.
* [ ] Destroy one of your own organs.
* [ ] Draw a replacement card.
* [ ] Does **NOT** count as your turn.

---
